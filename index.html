About the café

A Table Close to Home

The Daily Grind started as a single espresso machine and four chairs. Today it is still a small room where the barista knows your order and the pastries come out of the oven before the doors open. We roast in small batches each week and buy from growers on the Mambilla Plateau.

  • Opened2019, Lekki Phase 1
  • BeansRoasted weekly, single origin
  • Seating32 seats plus a small terrace
  • Good to knowWi-Fi, power sockets, backup generator
